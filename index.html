About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the College of Information Sciences and Technology at The Pennsylvania State University, where I am co-advised by Professor Suhang Wang and Professor Xiang Zhang. I was an Applied Scientist Intern at AWS AI in California. I received my B.E. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Chongqing University.

I'm actively seeking internship opportunities for Summer 2027. I'd love to connect if you think there might be a fit with your team!
